About the Founder Attiya Batool

Mysterious was created by Attiya Batool, an applied psychology student who believes learning about the mind should feel calm, kind and welcoming. She built this platform as a warm, safe place where anyone — from curious beginners to fellow students — can explore psychology through short notes, playful quizzes and friendly conversation.

As founder and admin, Attiya personally looks after every corner of Mysterious, so the community stays supportive and the content stays trustworthy. Welcome in — this universe was made for you. 🌿
